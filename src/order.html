How to Place Your Order

Enjoy your favourite food at Campus Bites by following these simple steps.

Simple Steps to Order

Follow these four easy steps to complete your order.

Choose Your Food

Visit our menu and check the available food items. Select your favourite items and decide the required quantity.

Pay at the Counter

Tell the cashier your selected items. Confirm your order and total bill, then pay at the cafeteria counter.

Collect Your Token

After payment, collect your order token or receipt. Keep it safe because your token number identifies your order.

Pick Up Your Order

Wait in the pickup area. When your token number is announced, show your token to the staff and collect your food.

Peak Hours Guidelines

The cafeteria may become busy during lunch breaks and between classes. Please follow these guidelines:

  • Allow extra time during lunch breaks.
  • Keep your payment ready before reaching the counter.
  • Follow the queue and wait for your turn.
  • Be patient during busy hours.

Pickup Rules

Follow these rules to collect your order smoothly:

  • Keep your order token safe.
  • Wait in the designated pickup area.
  • Listen carefully for your token number.
  • Do not crowd the serving counter.
  • Check your food before leaving.
  • Contact staff if there is a problem with your order.

Need Help With Your Order?

Our cafeteria staff is always ready to help you. Contact us if you have any questions.
